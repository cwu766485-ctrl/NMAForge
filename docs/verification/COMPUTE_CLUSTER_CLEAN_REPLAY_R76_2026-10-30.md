Compared with depth-2/no-prefetch/no-cache, depth-4/prefetch/cache is 1,698→1,187 cycles (30.1% fewer) and 512→384 bank reads (25% fewer). This is a one-cycle behavioral SPAD experiment, not SRAM timing or ASIC PPA. The 250 MHz column is an arithmetic normalization, not measured Fmax.

The real-top task timings in each UVM seed are identical: serial Matrix/CNM/SFU `565/217/457` cycles, total `1,239`; staged Matrix+CNM then dependent SFU `1,070` cycles (1.158×); and four-task queued parallel run `1,137` cycles against `1,696` serialized (1.492×). Completion IDs remain ordered; shared-SPAD contention is 32 cycles staged and 38 cycles in the four-task run. This is one fixed legal task set, not a general workload speedup claim.
