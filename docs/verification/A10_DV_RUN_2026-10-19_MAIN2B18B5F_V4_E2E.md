- The Makefile randomized-coverage target and URG merge gates both passed. Its E2E covergroup and all four per-transaction ARREADY, AWREADY, read-response-gap, and write-data-gap groups reported 100%.
- All 19 Makefile-gated functional points reached 100%. The additional `cp_error_position` report was also 2/2 (100%). Covered scenarios include descriptor order, SPAD layout/banks, invalid descriptors, FP16/BF16 K lengths and length cross, AXI read/ready/address gaps, DMA SLVERR position, active-compute reset, outstanding-DMA reset, accelerator-only reset with live DDR, recovery, watchdog and software-abort completion, and end-to-end result checking.

The two no-attempt properties are UVM RAL internal `uvm_reg_map::do_read` and `do_write` properties; they are not DUT assertion passes. All 3 AXI stall-to-accept cover properties matched.
- The 20 successful properties include testbench compute-activity monitors, AXI master protocol assertions, and a UVM component-name check; this is not a claim that 20 product-DUT assertions passed.
- The detached worktree was clean before and after the run. A post-run process check found no active VCS, simulator, or URG process.
