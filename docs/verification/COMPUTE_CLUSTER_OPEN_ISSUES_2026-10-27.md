| Matrix | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | — |

| Mixed FP | 1 | — | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | — |
| Conv | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | combined SPAD 7 | — | — |
| KV | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | combined SPAD 7 | — | — |

Cause codes: `0 NONE`, `1 CONFIG`, `2 OPCODE` where supported, `3 ABORT`, `4 WATCHDOG`, `5 PARITY`, `6 PROTECTION`, `7 merged SPAD error`, `8 SFU NUMERIC`, and `9–15 RESERVED`. Conv and KV use code 7 when their SPAD interface merges parity/protection; code 4 remains watchdog. Same-cycle priority is per Engine in SPEC v1.4.
