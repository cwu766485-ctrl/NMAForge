## Recently closed RTL defect

**R73 CPLQ consumer-progress defect — closed at exact clean SHA `9d4e9ab`:** the generic AXI-Lite `cfg_locked` rule rejected `CPLQ_HEAD` writes while the parallel dispatcher was busy. With a full completion queue this prevented software from freeing a slot and could deadlock ordered retirement. R73 permits the software-owned `CPLQ_HEAD` write while busy, adds SPEC v1.8 wording, and verifies a real four-engine top sequence with full hold, busy-time head advance, completion-order preservation and tail wrap. Three clean seeds, Top Smoke, seeded top regression and 28/28 checker-sensitivity campaign pass. The issue is fixed; `REQ-PC-004` stays PASS-SCOPED and the overall sign-off remains HOLD.
